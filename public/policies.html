About Us

Don’t Block Me is a fun online friend-quiz game. You create a quiz about yourself, share the link with your friends, and they guess your answers for fun. There are no cash prizes, no betting and no real-money gaming. Operated by Flower.

Privacy Policy

We store only what the game needs: the names you type, the quiz questions and answers you create, scores, and an anonymous random visitor ID kept in your browser so we can count visits and plays. We do not read your contacts, location, photos or phone data. When payments are switched on, they are processed by Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets); we never see your card number, UPI PIN or bank details. We do not sell your data. To ask us to delete your data, email deviltwister520@gmail.com.

Terms of Use

The game is for entertainment only. Creating and playing quizzes is currently free during our launch period. In the future, creating a quiz may cost a small fee (planned at ₹5), which will always be shown clearly before you pay. Playing a friend’s quiz stays free. Do not use the game to harass or bully anyone, and do not put hateful, sexual or illegal content in questions or names. We may remove quizzes that break these rules. Scores are for fun and do not describe anyone’s real friendships.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

While the game is free, no payment is taken, so there is nothing to refund. When a paid option is introduced, each payment will unlock one quiz creation. If you were charged but could not create your quiz because of a technical problem, email deviltwister520@gmail.com with your payment ID and we will refund you within 7 working days. Completed quizzes are not refundable. There is no delivery of physical goods.
